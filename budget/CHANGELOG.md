# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- New Home Assistant sensors: budget consumed (%), end-of-month projection, categories over budget.
- `dashboard-card.yaml`: ready-made overview card for a Home Assistant dashboard.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Monthly budget by group, category and sub-line, with rollover, provisions and envelope transfers.
- Tricount-style expenses: paid by, split by income (frozen ratio per month), 50/50, 100 % one person or custom.
- Balance between the two people, with settle-up and history.
- Fixed expenses created on their day and confirmed with the real amount.
- Savings accounts, monthly contributions, balance snapshots, goals and charts.
- AI monthly recap with the Anthropic API (aggregated data only).
- JSON backup and restore, CSV export and import.
- Home Assistant sensors and an iOS Shortcut endpoint.
